Our Story & Space

More Than Just A Cafe.

Nestled at Sainath Apartment opposite the bustling Mahavir Shopping Mall in Mundra, Thapas Cafe was created as a cozy retreat from the everyday rush. We combine the friendly warmth of an independent neighborhood coffee shop with the appetizing menu of a full-fledged casual restaurant.

Whether you are meeting up with close friends, sharing an afternoon bite with your family, or seeking a quiet corner to sip an espresso, Thapas Cafe is designed to be your comfortable dining haven. We take pride in honest flavours, courteous hospitality, and an ambience that invites you to stay a little longer.
